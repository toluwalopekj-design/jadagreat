Project Overview

BINCOM BANK is an academic concept project exploring a complete visual identity system for a modern, digital-first banking institution. The project demonstrates comprehensive systems thinking across digital interfaces, environmental design, merchandise, and brand applications — positioning the brand as innovative, trustworthy, and future-oriented.

Project Brief: Design a cohesive visual identity system for a fintech-forward banking institution that communicates innovation, security, and accessibility across digital and physical touchpoints.

Design System Foundations

1. The Colour Strategy

The colour palette was engineered to communicate both trust and innovation. Deep Blue (#1E3A8A) establishes authority and security — traditional banking traits. Bright Cyan (#00B7C2) introduces energy and forward-thinking, signalling digital transformation. The gradient accent (orange to cyan) is used strategically across applications to create visual momentum.

Colour Decision: Rather than relying solely on banking blue, the system introduces a secondary accent colour that feels tech-native. This reinforces the "future of banking" positioning without abandoning institutional credibility.

2. Typography System

TT Norms Pro was chosen as the primary typeface — geometric, modern, and highly legible across scales. The system uses clear weight hierarchy: Bold for headlines, Medium for subheadings, and Regular/Light for body copy. This ensures consistency from business cards to billboards.

Typography Decision: TT Norms Pro's geometric character reinforces the "smart banking" narrative. The typeface feels contemporary without sacrificing the formality required for financial services.

3. Logo System

The logo exists in two primary lockups: stacked (for app icons and small applications) and horizontal (for primary branding and print). Both maintain clear spacing and hierarchy. The "BANK" wordmark receives slightly lighter weight, allowing "BINCOM" to be the primary focal point.

Logo Decision: The dual-lockup system ensures flexibility without compromising brand recognition. The app icon treatment (rounded square with gradient accent) feels native to iOS/Android ecosystems while maintaining brand consistency.

Digital-First Applications

Mobile & App Interfaces

The system prioritizes digital experiences. The mobile app UI demonstrates intuitive navigation with the cyan accent guiding user actions. The credit card design integrates the gradient, creating a premium feel while maintaining clarity for cardholder information.

Mobile App Interface

ATM Interface Design

Digital Design Decision: The system feels equally at home on a 6-inch screen and a 24-inch ATM display. Colour contrast is maintained for accessibility, while the geometric logo scales seamlessly across all sizes.

Website & Web Experience

The website landing page uses the deep blue as primary background with cyan accents for CTAs and interactive elements. The "About Us" section introduces photography that communicates connectivity and global reach.

Print & Environmental Design

Stationery & Collateral

Business cards demonstrate refined simplicity — white front with cyan accent band, blue back. The tagline "Empowering Your Future with Smart Banking" reinforces positioning. The gradient accent runs along the card edge, creating a premium, intentional detail.

Print Decision: Even small details like the gradient edge treatment signal intentionality. Every touchpoint reinforces the brand story of modern, trustworthy banking.

Out-of-Home & Scale

Billboards and event banners demonstrate how the system scales to large formats. Bold typography, clear hierarchy, and strategic use of the gradient ensure the brand reads from distance while maintaining sophistication.

Environmental Design Decision: The colour system reads clearly at distance. The cyan accent is visible even in low contrast situations, ensuring brand recognition in real-world conditions.

Brand Extensions & Merchandise

Promotional & Wearable Applications

The system extends naturally to t-shirts, tote bags, and merchandise. Consistent use of the stacked logo and colour palette maintains brand cohesion while allowing flexibility in application. Merchandise feels premium, not promotional.

System Strengths & Insights

What Works

Cohesion at Scale: The system maintains visual consistency from a 32x32px app icon to a billboard. This is achieved through disciplined use of three core elements: logo, colour, and typography.

Digital-Native Thinking: Rather than adapting a traditional bank logo to digital, the system was designed for digital first. The rounded square app icon, gradient accents, and cyan as a primary colour all feel native to app ecosystems.

Clear Hierarchy: Every application has a visual hierarchy: logo, headline, supporting copy. This ensures clarity regardless of context — billboard, business card, or app interface.

Academic Context

This project demonstrates comprehensive systems thinking: the ability to design not just a logo, but a complete identity system that functions across dozens of applications. It shows fluency in colour theory, typography, digital interface design, and environmental graphics.

Learning Value: BINCOM BANK proves the designer understands brand systems architecture — how individual elements (logo, colour, type) combine to create cohesive brand experiences at any scale.
