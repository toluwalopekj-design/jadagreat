Case Study
RAEBEL — The "Mini Skirt" Campaign

The Challenge: Engineering a Cultural Anthem

Launching a debut single requires more than just good audio; it requires a movement. For emerging Afro Dancehall artist RAEBEL, the objective for her March 2025 release was ambitious: transform a track into a fierce, unapologetic feminist cultural statement. We needed to craft a narrative that didn't just introduce an artist, but actively disrupted the space and commanded attention.

The Strategy: Unapologetic Femininity

We anchored the campaign in a bold, defiant brand positioning, giving RAEBEL a visual and strategic voice that refused to be ignored. To maximize reach and resonance, we segmented our targeting into three distinct, high-impact personas:

  • The City Girl: Driven, trend-setting, and fiercely independent.
  • The Activist Gen Z: Culturally aware, vocal, and ready to champion empowering narratives.
  • The Fashion Rebel: Edgy, experimental, and drawn to disruptive aesthetics.

To capture these audiences, we engineered a comprehensive three-phase campaign architecture spanning pre-release anticipation, the launch event, and sustained post-release cultural integration.

The Visual Language: Street-Style Edge Meets Royal Defiance

The visual identity needed to perfectly mirror the track's energy. We built a color palette centered on a striking dichotomy: the grounded, rich intensity of Old Wine Red contrasted against the vibrant, unapologetic pop of Cinderella Pink. Paired with brutalist, statement-making typography, the visual direction drew heavy inspiration from edgy Nigerian and diaspora street style. The resulting moodboard was loud, hyper-feminine, and deeply rooted in modern Afro-culture.
